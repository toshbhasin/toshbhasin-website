Thinking

Technology changes systems.
Transformation changes
how institutions think.

I believe the most important transformation questions are rarely about which technology to buy. They are about what we want people to experience, what decisions we want to improve, and what the institution needs to become.

Ideas I work by

01
Start with the experience.

Technology should follow the experience we want to create, not define it.

02
Connect before you automate.

Broken processes do not become better simply because they become digital.

03
Build intelligence into decisions.

The real opportunity for AI is not more technology, but better decisions at every level.
