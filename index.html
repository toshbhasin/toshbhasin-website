How I sequence AI adoption

The four-phase maturity model I use to move an institution from pilots to AI-native operations, without outrunning its governance.

01

Assisted

AI supports individual tasks — drafting, summarizing, search — with a human reviewing every output. Low risk, fast to deploy, builds literacy.

02

Advisory

AI recommends decisions across a defined workflow — admissions triage, resource planning — but a person still approves the action.

03

Adaptive

Systems begin to act within guardrails, learning from outcomes and adjusting — student support routing, dynamic scheduling — under active oversight.

04

Autonomous

Agentic systems execute defined processes end to end, with governance built into the architecture rather than bolted on after.
